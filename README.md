# The Afterlife of a Calling

*Work, worth, and the search for signs from Calvin to the present.*

A long-form essay that traces the Reformed "practical syllogism", the inference from a fruitful life to assurance of salvation, through five eras: Calvinist, Enlightenment, self-made, therapeutic, and contemporary. It argues that the conclusion sought has stayed constant, the evidence has changed, and the authority behind the major premise has disappeared.

Live: https://techmore.github.io/protestant-work-ethic/

## Structure
- `index.html` is the essay, generated from a source file with `[[sidenote]]` markers
- `evidence.html` lists every quantitative claim with its source and caveats
- `historiography.html` covers the Weber debate
- `timeline.html` gives the five eras with key texts
- `sources.html` is the bibliography; entries live in `_data/bib.yml` (books link to Amazon, papers to DOIs)
- The old chapter URLs redirect to the matching essay sections

Set `amazon_tag` in `_config.yml` to add an Amazon Associates tag.
